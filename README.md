# CS-250

### **How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?*
I believe in order to understand what the user it looking for, we must understand the **who**, **what**, and **why** of a feature. Then, the stories can be formatted in the, **"As a ____, I want to _____ so that _____"**

### **How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?*
Developing a program should be approached in an organised manner. In an Agile framework flexibility is extremely important. The overall development would be broken down into smaller "sprints" and all teams should have good communication with one another. There are some tools that can help with this such as Azure Boards and Jira. I hope to incorporate things like daily standups, sprint planning and estimation techniques such as affinity grouping. 

### **What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?*
I believe a good developer isn't necessarily the most skilled on the team, or the one with the most seniority or experience. A good developer is one that hears what the client wants/needs, delivers that product and adapts to any changes that come along. A good developer also is a team player. Working well with others and understanding where you are weaker and where your strengths are is very important.
